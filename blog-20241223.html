What is an eFoiling or Seabob Rental?

eFoiling

Picture gliding effortlessly above the water on a state-of-the-art hydrofoil board powered by an electric motor. No waves are required, just pure fun as you soar across Sarasota’s stunning waters. eFoiling offers a blend of surfing, flying, and cutting-edge technology for an unforgettable experience.

Seabob

Dive beneath the surface with a Seabob, a high-performance water scooter that lets you navigate underwater with ease. It’s the ultimate way to explore Sarasota’s marine life up close, adding an adventurous twist to your beach day.
