What is eFoiling?

eFoiling is like riding a magic carpet above water! You stand on an electric hydrofoil board that uses a wing-shaped foil to lift you above the water’s surface. A whisper-quiet electric motor provides propulsion, and you control the speed with a handheld Bluetooth remote.
