Frequently Asked Questions for eFoil Adventures

chevron_right What is eFoiling and how does it work?

eFoiling, also known as electric hydrofoiling, is an exciting water sport that combines the thrill of surfing with cutting-edge electric technology. At Opua Adventures, eFoiling offers a unique experience where riders glide smoothly above the water's surface on a specially designed board powered by an electric motor.

How Does eFoiling Work?

An eFoil board is equipped with a hydrofoil—a wing-like structure mounted on a mast beneath the board—that lifts the rider above the water as the board gains speed. The electric motor, controlled by a wireless remote, powers a propeller attached to the hydofoil. When the motor is engaged, it propels the board forward, allowing the hydrofoil to generate lift and elevate the rider above the water, reducing drag and enabling a smooth, almost silent ride.

The rider controls the speed and direction with the remote, making eFoiling accessible to beginners while still offering a thrilling experience for advanced riders. Because the board hovers above the water, riders experience less resistance and can enjoy a unique sensation of flight over the waves.

At Opua Adventures, eFoiling is a safe, eco-friendly, and exhilerating way to explore the beautiful waters of Sarasota and beyond. Whether you're a seasoned water sports enthusiast or trying it for the first time, eFoiling promises an unforgettable adventure.

chevron_right Can I do this if I'm not a strong swimmer?

Yes, but you should feel comfortable in the water with a life vest. Our instructors are trained in water safety, and we stay in shallow, calm areas during lessons. Seabobs are especially great for those less confident in open water.

chevron_right What happens if I fall in the water?

Falling is part of learning and is actually quite fun! The water around Sarasota is warm and beautiful. You'll be wearing a life jacket for safety, and our instructors are always nearby. The eFoil automatically stops when you fall, and getting back on is easy with practice.

chevron_right Where do eFoiling lessons take place?

We operate in beautiful locations around Sarasota Bay, primarily at New Pass and Bird Key. These locations offer crystal clear waters, stunning views, and perfect conditions for learning. The exact location is provided the night before or the day of your adventure based on weather and water conditions.

chevron_right How long do eFoil lessons take place?

Our eFoil adventures are 90 minutes total, which includes beach instruction, water training, and independent flying time. This gives you plenty of time to learn the basics and experience the thrill of flying above the water.

chevron_right What happens if the weather is bad on the day of my booking?

We closely monitor Sarasota weather and will contact you if conditions aren't ideal. You'll have the option to reschedule or receive a full refund—your choice. Safety always comes first.

Frequently Asked Questions for Seabob Adventures

chevron_right What is a Seabob and how does it work?

Seabobs are sleek and powerful underwater scooters that propel you through the water like a dolphin! They have intuitive controls and varying speed options, allowing you to dive, surface, and explore underwater with ease. You simply hold on and steer—the Seabob does all the work!

chevron_right Is it safe for children and beginners?

Yes! Seabobs are perfect for ages 10 and up. We provide comprehensive safety instruction and high-quality goggles. Children must be comfortable in water and have basic swimming ability. Life jackets are available if desired, and we stay in areas where you can stand if needed.

chevron_right Do you really deliver to the beach for free?

Yes! We provide free delivery to Siesta Key Beach (Access 5) and Lido Beach. We bring the Seabob, safety equipment, and instruction right to you. For other locations, we offer custom delivery for an additional fee. This convenience is part of what makes our service special.

chevron_right Can multiple people share one Seabob?

Absolutely! Seabobs are perfect for sharing. Many families and groups take turns, allowing everyone to experience the underwater adventure. The 60-minute session gives multiple people plenty of time to explore. We can also provide multiple Seabobs for larger groups.

chevron_right How deep can I go with a Seabob?

For safety and optimal experience, we recommend staying in shallow to moderate depths (typically 3-15 feet). The crystal-clear waters around Siesta Key and Lido Beach provide excellent visibility at these depths, and you'll see plenty of marine life!

chevron_right What should I bring for a Seabob Adventure?

Just bring swimwear, a towel, and sunscreen (reef-safe preferred)! We provide high-quality goggles, life jackets (if desired), and all necessary safety equipment. The Seabob and instruction are included in your adventure.

Safety & Requirements

chevron_right What are the age and weight requirements?

eFoils: Ages 14+ (max 245 lbs)
Seabobs: Ages 10+ (no weight limit)

chevron_right What safety equipment do you provide?

We provide all necessary safety equipment: life jackets for all water activities and high-quality goggles for Seabobs. All equipment is regularly inspected, cleaned, and maintained to the highest standards.

chevron_right Do I need to know how to swim?

Basic swimming ability is required for eFoil and Seabob adventures. You don't need to be an expert swimmer, but you should be comfortable in water and able to swim a short distance. We provide life jackets and stay in areas where you can stand if needed.

chevron_right What happens in case of emergency?

All our instructors are trained in water safety and first aid. We carry emergency communication devices and have established protocols for various situations. Our locations are chosen for safety and accessibility to emergency services if needed.

chevron_right Are there any medical conditions that would prevent participation?

Please inform us of any medical conditions, medications, or physical limitations when booking. While most people can safely enjoy our adventures, certain heart conditions, pregnancy, or severe mobility issues may require special consideration or prevent participation.

Weather & Cancellations

chevron_right What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety is our top priority. We monitor weather and water conditions closely and will reschedule if conditions aren't suitable. Weather-related cancellations receive a full refund or free rescheduling with no penalties. We typically make weather decisions 2-4 hours before your scheduled adventure.

chevron_right What if weather conditions cancel adventures?

We cancel for lightning, severe storms, high winds (over 15 mph for eFoils), rough water conditions, or poor visibility. Light rain usually doesn't cancel water activities since you're getting wet anyway!

chevron_right How much notice do you give for weather-related cancellations?

We monitor weather continuously and aim to give as much notice as possible. Typically, we'll contact you 2-4 hours before your adventure if conditions look questionable. For obvious severe weather, we may cancel further in advance.

chevron_right What's the best time of the year to visit?

Sarasota is beautiful year-round! Peak season (December through April) offers the most consistent weather but is busier. Summer months (May through September) can have afternoon thunderstorms but also fewer crowds and warmer water. Fall and spring offer great weather and moderate crowds.

chevron_right Can adventures be rescheduled multiple times?

Yes! We understand Florida weather can be unpredictable. Weather-related rescheduling is always free and can be done multiple times if needed. We'll work with you to find suitable conditions and timing that works for your schedule.
