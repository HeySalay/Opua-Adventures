Sarasota Boat Tours

Climb aboard our comfortable 20-foot tritoon and spend a few hours cruising the beautiful waters around Sarasota and Bradenton with your own knowledgeable captain. Whether you want to relax on a sandbar, search for dolphins, explore the coastline, or simply turn up the music and soak up the Florida sunshine, you decide how you want to spend your time.

Our private captained tours are limited to 6 passengers, making them perfect for families, couples, friends, bachelor and bachelorette groups, or anyone looking for a more personal experience on the Suncoast.


What Your Day Could Look Like:

Every trip is a little different. Depending on the weather, tides, and water conditions, your captain can take you to some of the area's favorite spots for:
